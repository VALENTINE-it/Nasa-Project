## 1. Flight Phase Physiological Baselines

AURA-MED calibrates its autonomous physiological models against published NASA flight phase longitudinal data:

### Phase 1: Pre-Flight Baseline ($1.0g$ Terrestrial Gravity)
* **Resting Heart Rate:** $60\text{ – }68\text{ bpm}$ (High parasympathetic tone, robust baroreceptor responsiveness).
* **Blood Pressure:** $118/74\text{ mmHg}$ (Normal arterial compliance).
* **Blood Oxygen ($SpO_2$):** $98\text{ – }99\%$ at sea-level atmospheric pressure ($101.3\text{ kPa}$).
* **Body Temperature:** $36.6\text{ – }36.8°\text{C}$ (Standard terrestrial convective cooling).
* **Bone Demineralization Rate:** $0.0\%/\text{month}$ (Normal Wolff's Law bone turnover equilibrium).
* **Salivary Cortisol:** $10.0\text{ – }14.0\text{ nmol/L}$ (Normal morning circadian peak).

### Phase 2: In-Flight Microgravity ($0.00g$ Deep-Space Mars Transit)
* **Cephalic Fluid Shift:** Within 48 hours of orbital entry, **$1.5\text{ to }2.0\text{ Liters}$ of interstitial and venous fluid shift from the lower limbs into the thoracic cavity and head**.
* **Resting Heart Rate:** Shifts upward to **$74\text{ – }78\text{ bpm}$** (Compensatory response to decreased circulating stroke volume and altered baroreflex sensitivity).
* **Blood Pressure:** Shifts to **$124/80\text{ – }136/86\text{ mmHg}$** (Elevated central venous pressure and intracranial hypertension; risk of Spaceflight Associated Neuro-ocular Syndrome - SANS).
* **Body Temperature ("Space Fever"):** Rises by **$+0.4\text{ to }+0.6°\text{C}$ to $37.1\text{ – }37.3°\text{C}$**. Zero-G eliminates natural convective air currents, severely impairing body heat dissipation.
* **Bone Mineral Loss:** Demineralization of **$1.0\text{ to }1.5\%/\text{month}$** in trabecular and cortical bone without T2 treadmill and ARED countermeasure compliance.
* **Immune Suppression & Latent Virus Reactivation:** Deep-space cosmic radiation and neuroendocrine stress downregulate T-lymphocytes ($CD4^+/CD8^+$ ratio drops to $1.2\text{ – }1.6$), triggering reactivation of dormant childhood herpesviruses (Epstein-Barr Virus [EBV] and Varicella Zoster Virus [VZV]) shed into saliva ($> 50\text{ copies/mL}$).

### Phase 3: Post-Flight Planetary Surface Recovery (Mars $0.38g$ / Earth $1.0g$)
* **Orthostatic Intolerance:** Upon re-entering a gravity field, fluids immediately drain into the legs. Astronauts suffer acute postural hypotension ($BP < 90/60\text{ mmHg}$) and tachycardia ($HR > 120\text{ bpm}$) upon standing.
* **Recovery Protocol:** Requires oral rehydration therapy ($1.0\text{L}$ chilled isotonic sodium citrate electrolyte solution) and inflatable Lower Body Negative Pressure (LBNP) or compression garments.
